# Research artifact availability — publication update

The research artifacts accompanying *Bounded Autonomy Through Environmental Composition: An Experimental Study of the GRAIL Runtime* are publicly available as **GRAIL Research version 1.0.0**.

The archived package includes the manuscript (version 1.8), experimental worlds, eleven original scenario records across six experiment families, supporting evidence, and reproducibility documentation.

- **Permanent archive:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23740177
- **Source release:** https://github.com/mamund/grail-research/releases/tag/v1.0.0

This publication update supersedes the provisional archival-status statement in Section 12 of manuscript version 1.8. It does not change the experimental results, limitations, or conclusions reported in that manuscript.

The research package has not been independently certified as clean-room reproducible.
